# gregpignataro.com Design Brief

Everything needed to run a design optimization pass on gregpignataro.com in one folder. Upload the zip to Claude Design or share the folder with a human designer.

## What is in here

00-PROMPT.md. The prompt to paste into Claude. Uses the /design skill pattern. Drives three world class options per priority section with confidence scores and risk notes.

01-current-site.html. The live site as of 2026-04-20. Full HTML, all styles inline.

02-ux-heatmap-report.md. Section by section UX walk with predicted scroll depth, bounce risk, commitment math, and a prioritized fix list. Context, not gospel.

03-brand-context.md. Visual identity at a glance. Palette, typography, voice, imagery rules, motion vocabulary.

04-target-reader.md. The ICP. Who is this page really for. What makes her stay, what makes her bounce.

05-success-criteria.md. Seven ordered tests a design option has to pass. Applied in order, ties broken by earlier criterion.

## Asset folders

photography/headshots. Five Greg headshots. Kodak Portra, warm, natural light. Use greg_warm_connect.jpg for the hero. Others are held back and available. Do not generate AI portraits of Greg.

photography/hero-variants. Eight prebuilt hero compositions. Four warm (hero_warm_*, hero_option_*), four with split layout (hero_sp1_*, hero_sp2_*). A and B versions in each.

photography/testimonials. Cover and avatar images for existing clients (Wendy, Lee, Ray) plus headshots for Anthony, John, Rahul, James. Use the cover images for video testimonial cards and avatars for the smaller social proof tiles.

photography/transparent-cutouts. Six PNGs of Greg with transparent backgrounds, numbered 01 through 06. Use for hero compositions where you want to layer Greg over a brand plate or gradient.

brand-plates. Four atmospheric background images. Warm gold and cream. Use as section backgrounds, hero backdrops, or inside the manifesto blocks. kintsugi-stone-gold-veins.png is the strongest rest image. hero-golden-rays-landscape.png works well under the hero.

badges. Four gold treatment badges. Guinness World Record, Mate trained, 15 years, D1 athlete. Use with restraint. One or two max on the hero or in a credibility strip.

identity. OG image, favicon, apple touch icon. Reference only. Do not redesign these in this pass.

## How to use this

Option A. Upload the zip to Claude Design.

gregpignataro-design-brief.zip (one level up from this folder). Upload, then paste 00-PROMPT.md as the instruction. Claude will have access to every asset in the zip.

Option B. Run the /design skill in Cowork.

cd into this folder. Invoke /design. Paste the prompt.

Option C. Manual handoff.

Share the folder or zip with a human designer. Point them at 00-PROMPT.md first. The prompt is written so it works with or without the skill.

## What will come back

Three HTML design options per priority section (Hero, Is This You, Social Proof, Apply CTA), plus one prose argument for overall page rhythm. Each option includes rationale, confidence score 0 to 100, and risk note. A summary table closes the reply.
