Three to start with

If you only have time for three.

Essay 01

The Identity You Spent Your Life Building

"The self you spent your whole life building was not put there by you. It was assembled by everyone who got to you before you could speak. The work is not to be a better version of that self. The work is to notice it was never the only one."

Essay 02

Eight Days in the Dark

"I went into a dark retreat because I wanted to see the floor underneath the floor. What I found was not a revelation. It was a quiet sentence I had been running from for thirty years. The dark did not give me anything. It took something I no longer needed."

Essay 03

What Pain Is, Really

"Pain is not the message. It is the messenger. Most of the bodywork industry has spent forty years arguing about the messenger. The message is almost always the same and almost nobody is listening for it."
